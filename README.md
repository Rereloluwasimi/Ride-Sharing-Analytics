# Ride-Sharing-Analytics
A ride-sharing analytics project using Excel to explore customer behavior, driver performance, pricing strategies, and operational efficiency.

Data tells stories, and this project is about uncovering the story behind 10,000 ride-sharing trips.
Using Microsoft Excel for data cleaning, analysis, and PowerBi fror dashboard creation, I explored how customers use the platform, how drivers perform, and which operational factors have the biggest impact on revenue and customer experience. The goal wasn't just to create charts—it was to answer real business questions and turn data into actionable insights. The analysis is based on a dataset of 10,000 ride records covering customer behavior, operational efficiency, and financial performance.

# What I Explored
Customer travel patterns across locations and time periods

Peak demand by weekday, weekend, and time of day

Vehicle preferences and fleet utilization

Driver performance and experience

Ride duration and operational efficiency

Revenue performance and surge pricing effectiveness

Cancellation and no-show trends

Payment method preferences

The impact of weather and traffic on ride experience

# Key Insights

Some of the findings challenged common assumptions:

Sedans and SUVs account for about 70% of all rides, making them the backbone of the platform's operations.

Evening is consistently the busiest period, regardless of weekday or weekend.

Driver experience showed little relationship with customer ratings or completion rates.

Surge pricing increased revenue volume simply because more rides were under surge, but it did not significantly increase the average fare 
per ride.

Weather and traffic had surprisingly little impact on ride duration, driver ratings, or fares.

Around 15% of rides ended in cancellations or no-shows, suggesting opportunities to investigate factors beyond weather and payment methods.

# Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations could help improve operational efficiency and customer experience:

Increase driver availability during evening hours to meet peak demand.

Review and optimize the current surge pricing strategy, as it is not significantly increasing average fare per ride.

Focus driver coaching on low-performing individuals rather than experience level, since experience alone does not 
predict service quality.

Investigate other causes of ride cancellations and no-shows, such as wait times and driver response times, as weather and payment methods have minimal impact.

Continue prioritizing Sedan and SUV vehicles in fleet planning while monitoring the adoption of electric vehicles over time.
